TEACHER ZONE · answer key & lesson notes
  • Target language: always, often, usually, sometimes, hardly ever, never.
  • Prepositions: next to, between, opposite, across from, behind, in front of, above, below, underneath, near, inside.
  • Visuals: Levels 2 and 3 use a campus map (the road separates the rows, so “opposite” is clear) and a room picture. Every spatial answer can be checked in the image; answer positions are shuffled each time.
  • Teaching note: “hardly ever” means almost never. Frequency adverbs usually go before the main verb, but after the verb “be”.
  • Writing assessment: look for a clear routine, at least three frequency adverbs, and accurate place descriptions. Accept sensible personal answers.
